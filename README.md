# Storm's Open Menu

Storm's Open menu is a submenu/script for GTA 5/Online Kidions Moddest Menu.

Download the file [here](Storm's%20Open%20Menu%20V2.lua) or on the site!

Visit https://stormxxboy.com/projects/storms-open-menu/ for more information!
<h1>Storm's Open Menu</h1>
        <h2>Storm's Open menu is a submenu/script for GTA 5/Online Kiddions Moddest Menu.</h2>
        <h1>Features</h1>
        <p>-Easy to read.</p>
        <p>-Easy to use.</p>
        <p>-Open source.</p>
        <p>-Lots of modules.</p>
        <h1>Modules</h1>
        <h2>Self Modules</h2>
        <p>-Semi Godmode- Godmode but you can still be one shotted.<p>
        <p>-Remove Cops- Removes your wanted level.<p>
        <p>-6 Stars- Gives yourself 6 stars.<p>
        <p>-Drop Money- Drops money on you.<p>
        <p>-Safer Drop Money- Drops money on you safer but for less profit.<p>
        <p>-Teleport Forward- Teleports you forward.<p>
        <h2>Weapon Modules</h2>
        <p>-Force Gun- When you hit something it goes flying.</p>
        <p>-Boom Gun- Fire's a tiny explosion.</p>
        <p>-Nuke Gun- Fire's a big explosion.</p>
        <p>-Instant Kill Gun- Kill's anything instantly.</p>
        <p>-Rapid Fire- Shoots faster than normal.</p>
        <p>-Long Range- Makes your gun shoot further.</p>
        <h2>Vehicle Modules</h2>
        <p>-Car Godmode- Sets Godmode for your car.</p>
        <p>-Gravity Toggle- Switches to 0 gravity and gravity.</p>
        <p>-Unstuck Car- When your car is stuck spam this a little bit to get unstuck.</p>
        <p>-Fast Car- Makes your car very fast.<p>
        <p>-Infinite Car Speed Limit- Sets your car speed limit infinite.<p>
        <h2>Troll Modules</h2>
        <p>-Human Drop- Drops humans on you.</p>
        <p>-Skydive- Launches you in the air.</p>
        <p>-When Cars Fly- Makes a random car fly and teleports you to that car.</p>
        <p>-Car Drop- Drops a car on you.</p>
        <p>-Kill Car- Kill's your current car.</p>
        <p>-Fragile Car- Makes your car fragile.</p>
        <p>-Snow Toggle- Switches the world from snow to normal.</p>
        <p>-Become Tiny- Switches you from tiny to big.</p>
        <h2>Playerlist</h2>
        <p>-Lists all players with information.</p>
        <h2>Reports Menu</h2>
        <p>-View your reports.</p>
        <h1>Hotkeys</h1>
        <p>-F9 = Kill's all NPC's</p>
        <p>-F10 = Set's a local teleport position.</p>
        <p>-F11 = Teleports you to the local position.</p>
        <p>-F12 = When your car is stuck spam this a little bit to get unstuck.</p>
        <p>-Numpad 1 = Give's you a speed boost.</p>
        <p>-Numpad 3 = Heal's you and your car.</p>
